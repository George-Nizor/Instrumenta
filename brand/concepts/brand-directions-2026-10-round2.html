What to decide

  1. An icon style, or two to combine. Freestanding for the app icon with blueprint for loading animations works, for example.
  2. A type pairing. Mixing roles across pairings is fine; Ludere's script page stays Courier whatever you choose.
  3. The glyphs themselves are still first drafts. Once the style is set, each one gets drawn properly at 16, 24 and 48 px.
