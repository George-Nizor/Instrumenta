# Dimensio glyph, 2026-10-15

These previews record how Forge3D's cube became Dimensio's measured cube. The decision is in
[`docs/suite/decisions.md`](../../../docs/suite/decisions.md); the glyph itself is drawn in
`brand/icons/instrumenta-icons.js`.

- `glyph-sizes.png` shows the generated icon at 256, 64, 48, 32, 24 and 16 px, then 32, 24 and 16 px
  enlarged, beside the rest of the suite and the organ, on dark and light grounds.
- `motion.png` shows the hover motion as stills: the arrows draw in and extend again, height first,
  then width, then depth.
- `drafts.png` shows three rejected drafts beside the chosen one. Filled arrows (A and C) were
  heavier than the cube's own lines and turned to smudges at 32 to 64 px. Triangle heads (B) read as
  dark wedges on a dark ground. Long extension lines (A) crowded the cube.
