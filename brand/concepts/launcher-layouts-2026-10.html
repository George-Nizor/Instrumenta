My recommendation

D, the library. The launcher now does more than open apps: updates, storage, readiness, the journal. As dialogs those features feel bolted on and slow; as pages in a sidebar they are first-class, and there is room for whatever comes next. It keeps the stage you liked as the detail panel, and the list scales past eight apps where the grid and the dock start to strain. If the launcher should stay a pure "pick an app and go" front door, B is the better choice.
