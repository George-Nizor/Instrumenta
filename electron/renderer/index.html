      About

      Instrumenta

      instrumenta, plural of instrumentum: tools, the things you make with.

      A suite of local creative tools. Everything runs on this computer; nothing you make is sent anywhere unless an app says so. Instrumenta installs the apps, keeps them up to date, and checks every download against its published checksum.

      Instrumenta is MIT licensed. Each app carries its own licence: LearnChess is GPL-3.0, the others MIT unless their repository says otherwise. The typefaces are Fraunces, Commissioner and Spline Sans Mono, under the SIL Open Font License.
